📖 Unit 10: Holidays — Classroom Language Helper

Use these useful expressions and grammar rules to survive your holiday!

1st Action (Earlier in the Past)
PAST PERFECT: had + past participle
"The plane had left at 10:00..."
➔
2nd Action (Later in the Past)
PAST SIMPLE: arrived / found / saw
"...when we arrived at 10:30."

📌 Holiday Collocations

  • • book a flight / a hotel room / a table
  • • check in at the airport / reception desk
  • • get lost / sunburned / on a plane
  • • go abroad / sightseeing / hiking / cycling
  • • hire a car / a tour guide / skis
  • • stay at a hotel / on a campsite / at home

😮 -ED vs -ING Adjectives

  • • -ED = How a person feels:
    "I am bored / excited / worried / tired / amazed / annoyed."
  • • -ING = What a thing is like:
    "The trip is boring / exciting / worrying / tiring / amazing."

💬 Asking & Suggesting (10d)

  • • "I'm interested in visiting..."
  • • "Do you know the opening times?"
  • • "Could you tell me the price?"
  • • "How often does the bus go?"
  • • "How about taking the bus?"
  • • "You could take a taxi."
