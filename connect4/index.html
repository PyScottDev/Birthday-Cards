How to Play Verb Connect Four

Verb Connect Four helps A1–A2 English learners master irregular past participles and the past perfect tense while playing classic Connect Four in two teams (Red Team vs. Yellow Team).

5-Step Classroom Turn Sequence

  1. Step 1 – Select a Verb: The active team chooses an available base verb from the Irregular Verb Bank (for example, "take").
  2. Step 2 – Answer Orally: The student says the target form aloud:
    • Mode A (Irregular Verbs): Say the past participle aloud (e.g., "taken").
    • Mode B (Past Perfect): Say "had" + past participle aloud (e.g., "had taken").
  3. Step 3 – Teacher Checks the Answer: The teacher clicks Reveal Answer to show the written answer on the screen, then clicks Correct or Incorrect:
    • If Incorrect: The team loses its turn, play passes to the other team, and the verb stays available for future turns.
    • If Cancel: Cancels the choice without using the verb so the same team can pick a different verb.
    • If Correct: The team earns the right to drop their counter!
  4. Step 4 – Choose a Column: Click a column button (or any space in that column) on the blue board. A coloured counter displaying the past participle drops to the lowest empty space.
  5. Step 5 – Next Turn: Once the counter lands, that verb is permanently retired for the rest of the game, and the turn switches to the other team.

Winning the Game

Connect four counters of your team's colour horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win! If all 42 spaces fill up—or all verbs in the selected difficulty pool are used—the game ends in a draw.
